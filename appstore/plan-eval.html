  • Chiffonade 15 basil leaves into ribbons in under 90 seconds without bruising the leaves.
  • Julienne two carrots into matchsticks in under 4 minutes and blanch them for 90 seconds, checking for even doneness.

Day 3: Pan Sauce Mastery

  • Sear a chicken breast 4 minutes per side, deglaze the pan with 1/4 cup stock, and reduce by half in 3 minutes.
  • Whisk in 1 tablespoon cold butter off heat for 30 seconds to emulsify the sauce until glossy.
  • Plate the chicken with sauce spooned over and taste-adjust with a pinch of salt, noting the change on paper.

Day 4: Protein Temperature Control

  • Sear a steak 3 minutes per side, insert a thermometer, and pull it at exactly 125°F for medium-rare.
  • Rest the steak 5 minutes under foil, then slice against the grain into 1/4-inch slices.
  • Cook a salmon fillet to 125°F internal in 8 minutes using the same thermometer for comparison.

Day 5: Speed Mise en Place

  • Cook all five components simultaneously, finishing within 25 minutes, using separate timers per burner.
  • Plate the full dinner in under 2 minutes and photograph the finished plate.
